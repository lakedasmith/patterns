Basketball Jersey

Hook: 4.25mm, 4.5mm | Yarn: 4
FRONT
Ch 81/46cm. Ch 1 for turning chain
Rows 1-5: sc, ch1, sk 1, repeat. Ch 1, turn. Ch 3 after Row 5
Row 6-35: DC across. Ch 3 (81)
Row 36: DC2tog at beg and end
(follow number chart to row 35. At row 36, begin decreases through row 39)

NECK
Divide in half. PM in middle (stitch #3 of 5 white)
Row 40: dc2tog, dc, dc2tog in last 2 sts before marker
Row 41: dc2tog, dc, dc2tog
repeat for rows 42 – 54 (15 rows/23cm/8 or 6 sts)
Repeat for other side

STRIPES
(main color)
sc at sm down sides through small and big holes. Change colors.
Row 1: sc in each sc
Row 2: sc across. Change colors
Row 3: sc across
repeat for other side, starting at bottom, ending at sm

BACK
Repeat FRONT without Neck, (54 rows ending with 43 sts)
Row 55: 7 dc, dc2tog (8) / 5 dc, dc2tog (6)
(other side) Row 55: dc2tog, 7dc / dc2tog, 5 dc (6)

Repeat STRIPES

JOINING
with smaller hook and WS facing join at shoulders with sc. With RS facing, join at hemline with ss.

SLEEVES
with bigger hook and main color (secondary color)
Ch 1, sc around the sleeves. Finish with ss and change colors.
Row 1: sc2tog, sc until last 2 sts, sc2tog, ss to 1st st. Ch 1, turn
Row 2: sc2tog, sc until last 2 sts, sc2tog, ss to 1st st. Change to main. Ch 1, turn
Row 3-5: sc2tog, sc until last 2 sts, sc2tog, ss to 1st st. Change colors. Ch 1, turn
Row 6-7: sc2tog, sc until last 2 sts, sc2tog, ss to 1st st and cut.
Repeat for other side

setup row | 1 2 | 3 4 5 | 6 7

NECK FRONT
with main color
Sc around, finish with ss and change colors. Ch 1
Round 1: sc inc, sc around, sc inc in last st. Ch 1, turn
Round 2: sc inc, sc around, sc inc in last st. Change color. Ch 1 and turn
Round 3: sc inc, sc around, sc inc in last sts. Ch 1 and FO. Sew together.
